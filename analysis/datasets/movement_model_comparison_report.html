🔍 Deep Physical Reverse-Engineering Insights

1. The Underlying Movement Mechanism

The empirical data decisively rejects pure linear Newtonian drag (RMSE: 64.19 px). The true motion in Spaceone.io is governed by bounded angular turn-rate kinematics (RMSE: 24.17 px, AIC: 3534.0).

  • Cruise Speed: Steady-state cruise speed is clamped at \(V_{max} pprox 14.34 ext{ px/tick}\) (\(358.5 ext{ px/s}\)).
  • Turn Rate Limit: The velocity vector heading rotates smoothly towards the target angle at a maximum rate of \(\omega_{max} pprox 0.139 ext{ rad/tick}\) (\(7.98^\circ ext{/tick}\) or \(pprox 200^\circ ext{/s}\)).

2. Mouse Heading vs Thrust Direction

In the network protocol, the client immediately transmits the player's target mouse heading (\(\hat{u}\)). However, the ship's actual velocity heading does not jump instantaneously. Instead:

  • The ship's visual sprite and velocity vector sweep along an arc constrained by \(\omega_{max}\).
  • During hard \(90^\circ\) and \(180^\circ\) turns, speed temporarily dips from \(13.76 ext{ px/tick}\) down to \(10.58 ext{ px/tick}\), preserving curvature rather than sliding on linear friction.
