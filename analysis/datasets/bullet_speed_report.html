🚀 Spaceone.io Physics Calibration: Bullet Speed & Speed Ratios

Empirical measurement and calibration of bullet speeds relative to ship cruise speeds across 41 original playback recording sessions (111k+ bullets analyzed).

🔬 Executive Summary & Core Invariant

Empirical Speed Ratio Model:
R(N) = V_bullet(N) / V_ship(N) = 2.0x - 3.0x (Median: ~2.35x)

Bullet Speed Scaling:
V_bullet(N) = 30.78 - 4.07 × ln(N) [px/tick]  ≡  769.5 - 101.9 × ln(N) [px/s]

In the original Spaceone.io engine, ShotThrust[N] and BaseThrust[N] are expressed directly in pixels/tick. In the remake engine, ships use scale factor BaseThrustConverter × MaxMomentumCoefficient = 0.0156. To preserve the exact authentic bullet-to-ship velocity ratio:

Engine Setting Adjustment:
Hook.ShotThrustConverter = (BaseThrustConverter × MaxMomentumCoefficient) / 10 = (0.0024 × 6.5) / 10 = 0.00156f (previously 0.0012f, a 30.0% increase to correct a 23.1% bullet deficit).
